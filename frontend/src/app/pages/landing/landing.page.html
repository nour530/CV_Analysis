Privacy

Your CV is yours

We built JobMatch AI so you never have to trade your personal data for a better CV.

  • Uploaded files are read and discarded; only the text is kept in your account.
  • AI features are off until you turn them on in Settings, and CV rewrites never include your contact details.
  • Delete your CV history at any time from Settings.
  • The ATS check and job matching run on our server, not in a third-party AI.
